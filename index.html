Chloe Ban

Environmental Science Student 🌱

I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of California, Riverside, pursuing a degree in Environmental Science. I am passionate about understanding the relationship between humans, animals, and the natural environment, as well as exploring ways to protect and preserve the planet for future generations. My academic interests include environmental sustainability, wildlife conservation, ecology, biodiversity, and the effects of human activities on natural ecosystems. Through my education at UC Riverside, I am developing a strong scientific foundation while learning to approach environmental challenges through research, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

As an Environmental Science student, I am particularly interested in understanding how environmental systems function and how changes in climate, pollution, land use, and resource consumption affect ecosystems. I believe that studying the environment requires an interdisciplinary perspective that combines biology, chemistry, ecology, and other scientific fields. My coursework has allowed me to explore scientific concepts, strengthen my analytical skills, and gain a better understanding of the complex processes that shape our environment. I am especially interested in learning how scientific research can be applied to real-world environmental issues and contribute to meaningful solutions.

My experience as a university student has helped me develop important skills in communication, organization, teamwork, and time management. Through laboratory coursework and academic projects, I have gained experience following scientific procedures, analyzing observations, interpreting results, and understanding the importance of accuracy in scientific research. These experiences have taught me to approach challenges with curiosity and patience while remaining open to learning new concepts and perspectives. I also value collaboration and believe that sharing ideas with others is an important part of academic and professional growth.

Beyond academics, I am interested in gaining hands-on experience through volunteer opportunities, internships, and research related to environmental conservation and animal welfare. I value opportunities that allow me to contribute to my community while expanding my understanding of the natural world. I believe practical experience is an important part of professional development because it allows students to apply what they learn in the classroom to real-world situations.

In the future, I hope to pursue opportunities that combine my interests in environmental science, wildlife, and conservation. I am particularly interested in the relationship between healthy ecosystems and animal populations, including how environmental changes influence wildlife habitats, biodiversity, and animal health. I hope to continue building my scientific knowledge and gaining experience that will help me contribute to environmental protection and the well-being of animals.

As I continue my education at UC Riverside, my goal is to challenge myself academically, explore different areas of environmental science, and develop the skills necessary for a career in a science-related field. I look forward to learning from professors, collaborating with other students, participating in research, and discovering new opportunities that align with my interests. I see my education as an ongoing process of growth and exploration, and I am excited to use the knowledge and experiences I gain to make a positive contribution to the environment and my community.
